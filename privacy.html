Privacy Policy

🔒 100% On-Device Privacy Architecture

VITT is designed as an offline-first, privacy-focused application. Your financial records, account balances, transaction history, and debt records (Khata) are stored locally on your device and are never uploaded, transmitted, or sold to any remote server.

This Privacy Policy explains how VITT (“we”, “our”, or “the app”), developed by Kilani Sai Nikhil, collects, uses, and protects your information when you use our mobile application.

1. Information We Collect and Receive

We believe in data minimization. We only collect the minimal information necessary for the application to function:

  • Personal Information (Optional): If you choose to sign in using Google Sign-In or Firebase Authentication, we receive your name and email address to manage your account profile. Account creation is completely optional; you may use the entire application in Guest Mode without providing any personal details.
  • Financial and Transaction Data (Local Only): Transaction amounts, categories, merchant names, budgets, and savings goals you enter manually or capture from statements are stored strictly in an encrypted local database (SQLite with AES-256 SQLCipher) on your device. We have zero access to your financial data.
  • Notification Data (Local Only): If you enable the optional Notification Listener feature, the app processes incoming payment and banking notifications on your device in real-time to automate expense logging. Personal chats, messages, and non-financial notifications are ignored and never saved or transmitted.
  • Crash Logs & App Diagnostics (Anonymous): We use Google Firebase Crashlytics to receive anonymous crash reports and diagnostics. These logs contain technical device information (e.g., OS version, device model, crash stack trace) and contain zero personal or financial information.

2. How We Use Your Information

Information collected is used strictly for the following purposes:

  • To provide and maintain the core functionality of the expense tracker.
  • To authenticate users who opt into Google Sign-In.
  • To diagnose application crashes, fix technical bugs, and improve software stability.
  • To perform on-device mathematical calculations, budget analytics, and local AI assistance.

3. Android Permissions Used

VITT requests only permissions strictly necessary for its features:

  • Notification Listener Access (BIND_NOTIFICATION_LISTENER_SERVICE): Opt-in access used strictly to detect financial SMS and UPI transaction alerts on-device.
  • Biometric Authentication (USE_BIOMETRIC): Used strictly to lock/unlock the app using your device's fingerprint or face recognition sensor. Biometric data never leaves the hardware security module.
  • Internet Access (INTERNET): Used strictly for optional user authentication and anonymous crash reporting.

4. Third-Party Service Providers

We use trusted third-party SDKs provided by Google LLC for infrastructure services. These providers operate under their respective privacy policies:

  • Google Play Services: Core application delivery and platform APIs.
  • Firebase Authentication: Secure user sign-in and identity tokens.
  • Firebase Crashlytics: Anonymous crash reporting and performance metrics.

5. Data Storage and Security

We implement robust technical and organizational security measures:

  • All local financial databases are encrypted using industry-standard AES-256 SQLCipher encryption.
  • Network communications (authentication and crash reports) are encrypted in transit using TLS 1.3 / HTTPS.
  • Sensitive app screens are protected with FLAG_SECURE to prevent unauthorized screenshots and screen recording.

6. User Rights, Data Deletion & Account Erasure

You have full control over your data at all times:

  • In-App Data Erasure: You can permanently wipe all stored transactions and ledger entries at any time by going to Profile → Data Management → Delete All Financial Data.
  • In-App Account Deletion: You can permanently delete your account and associated profile directly within the app via Profile → Data Management → Delete My Account.
  • Web/Email Deletion Request: If you wish to request deletion of your account and any associated authentication data via email, you may email us at vitt.companion@gmail.com with the subject line “Account Deletion Request”. Requests are processed within 48 hours.

7. Children's Privacy

VITT is not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe that a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately.

8. Financial & Legal Disclaimer

VITT is an automated financial tracking and educational utility. VITT is not a SEBI-registered investment advisor, portfolio manager, or banking institution. All calculations, budget insights, and AI responses are for informational purposes only and do not constitute financial, investment, legal, or tax advice.
