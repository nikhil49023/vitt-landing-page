## 2. Ideal Customer Profile (ICP) & Target Audience

### Primary Persona: The Everyday Indian UPI Spender ("Arjun", 24–35)
- **Profile:** Young professional, student, freelancer, or tech-savvy individual living in Tier 1/2 India.
- **Daily Behavior:** Makes 5–15 UPI transactions a day (Chai, Swiggy, Zepto, Uber, groceries, electricity bills).
- **Core Frustrations with Existing Apps (CRED, Walnut, Axio, Indmoney):**
  1. *Manual Entry Fatigue:* Too lazy or busy to write down every ₹20–₹300 UPI transaction manually.
  2. *Privacy Violations:* Hates granting invasive SMS permissions that read personal messages, OTPs, and bank balances.
  3. *Telemarketer Spam:* Getting spammed daily with unwanted personal loan and credit card calls after signing up for finance apps.
  4. *Subscription Paywalls:* Getting locked out of basic budgeting or CSV export features.
- **What Arjun Loves About VITT:**
  - Auto-captures UPI transactions the second they happen via notification listener.
  - Zero bank logins, zero SMS access, zero cloud accounts.
  - 100% offline private AI that answers spending questions without an internet connection.

## 4. Voice & Tone Guidelines
- **Empowering & Relatable:** Speak like a trustworthy, smart friend who understands Indian spending culture (Chai, Swiggy, Kirana stores, SIPs).
- **Benefit-First, Mechanism-Second:** Always lead with what the user gains before explaining how it works under the hood.
- **Jargon Policy:** Never mention raw compiler/hardware terms (LiteRT, TurboVec SIMD, minSdk 21) in user-facing headlines or marketing copy. Reserve engineering specs for the dedicated Technical Architecture modal.
