🛡️ 100% On-Device Storage 🔒 SQLCipher AES-256 📜 DPDP Act 2023 Compliant 🚫 Zero SMS Permissions

🔒 Sovereign Architecture Guarantee

VITT is engineered from first principles as an air-gapped, zero-knowledge personal finance companion. Your transaction records, account balances, split expenses, bank statements, and AI conversations are processed and stored exclusively inside your physical device hardware. VITT operates zero cloud ledger databases, zero analytics telemetry trackers, and zero remote data pipelines.

1. Data Controller & Sovereign On-Device Storage

Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act, 2023 and international privacy regulations:

  • Local-Only Database: All personal financial data resides inside an on-device SQLite database encrypted with AES-256 via SQLCipher. Cryptographic keys are generated and kept exclusively in the Android Keystore hardware security module.
  • No Cloud Account Required: You can utilize all features of VITT without creating an account, logging in, or sharing your email address or phone number.
  • Zero Data Transmission: We do not operate remote servers to collect, ingest, monetize, or profile your personal cashflow.

2. Notification Access & Automatic UPI Transaction Detection

VITT requests the Android system permission BIND_NOTIFICATION_LISTENER_SERVICE for the sole purpose of real-time expense logging:

  • Strict Application Whitelist: The on-device listener strictly filters notifications originating from verified banking, card issuer, and UPI payment apps (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, CRED, BHIM, Navi, HDFC, SBI, ICICI, Kotak, Axis, etc.).
  • WhatsApp Pay Detection: Only automated payment and settlement notifications from WhatsApp Pay containing valid transaction identifiers are parsed. All personal chats, text messages, contacts, and media are completely ignored and never read.
  • Zero SMS Permission: VITT does NOT request or use READ_SMS or RECEIVE_SMS permissions. VITT has zero technical access to your general SMS inbox or OTPs.
  • User Consent: Notification access is disabled by default on installation and requires explicit user activation in Android System Settings. You can revoke it at any time.

3. On-Device AI & Document OCR Parsing

VITT incorporates local machine learning and natural language processing capabilities:

  • Local LLM Inference: The AI financial reasoning companion runs entirely on-device using quantized LiteRT / SmallM models on your phone's processor/NPU. Your financial queries and database summaries never travel to external cloud AI providers.
  • Native PDF & Vision OCR: Bank statement PDFs, receipt scans, and bill images imported into VITT are parsed in memory on your device using on-device OCR and regex engines. No files are uploaded to external parsing APIs.

4. Split Ledgers (Khata) & 1-on-1 IOUs

VITT offers personal debt tracking and shared bill settlement capabilities:

  • Informal Private Tracking: Khata records are stored purely as personal notes on your device. VITT is not a lending institution, credit recovery agent, or credit information bureau (CIBIL/Experian).
  • Optional WhatsApp Reminders: Reminders are triggered only upon explicit user tap via Android intent links (wa.me) to the official WhatsApp client. No automated background messaging occurs.

6. User Rights & Complete Data Erasure (DPDP Act 2023)

In adherence with Section 12 of the DPDP Act 2023, you retain absolute ownership over your data:

📤 Export Ledger

Export your full transaction and budget history in open JSON format anytime via Profile > Data Export.

🗑️ Permanent Wipe

Permanently and irreversibly destroy all database tables, encryption keys, and cached files via Profile > Delete All Data.
